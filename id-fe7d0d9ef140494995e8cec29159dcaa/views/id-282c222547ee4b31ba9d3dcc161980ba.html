Motivation View - Requirements ()
Below is a comprehensive set of functional and non-functional requirements categorized across the six specified domains, directly incorporating the architectural standards, policies, and design patterns established in the reference architecture. 1. Business DomainFunctional RequirementsMulti-Channel Dissemination: The system shall provide 5 dedicated dissemination channels under the Universal Statistical Dissemination Protocol (USDP) to manage statistical data release. Automated Provisioning Operations: The portal shall offer self-service interfaces for users to request automated container cluster provisioning and namespace onboarding. Single System of Record Enforcement: The architecture shall maintain core internal databases as the sole deterministic system of record for all business processes. Institutional Authority Branding: The system shall attach official institutional metadata and provenance tags to all public data outputs. Multi-Cloud/On-Premises Service Routing: The business portal shall route service requests dynamically across on-premises legacy systems, private clouds, and public hyperscalers. Self-Service API Management: The platform shall enable business units to register, publish, and manage API endpoints for machine consumption.Business Process Mapping: The architecture shall map statistical dissemination workflows directly to SDMX standard models. Automated Resource Tracking: The system shall integrate IoT/RFID and digital asset tracking into operational workflows.Service Portfolio Integration: The platform shall display available service offerings from the enterprise service portfolio to end-users based on roles. Non-Functional RequirementsOperational Autonomy: The architecture shall preserve operational independence, ensuring critical business functions operate without single-vendor dependencies. Cost Trade-off Transparency: The platform shall track and report operational costs across single-cloud and multi-cloud deployment models. Service Availability: Business dissemination services shall maintain 99.9% uptime during peak statistical release periods.Business Continuity: System recovery time objectives (RTO) for core statistical publishing functions shall not exceed 4 hours.Usability Compliance: User interfaces shall adhere to standard usability and accessibility requirements for federal public platforms. Agility and Extensibility: Business capability designs shall accommodate evolving international statistical standards without requiring core re-engineering. User Onboarding Latency: Self-service namespace onboarding requests shall execute within 15 minutes of approval. 2. Data DomainFunctional RequirementsSDMX Data Modeling: The system shall utilize the Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange (SDMX) framework as its core information model. JSON-LD Schema Generation: The data architecture shall dynamically expose statistical datasets using structured JSON-LD schemas. Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration: The platform shall format metadata according to the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for machine ingestion. Dynamic Source Attribution: Every dataset payload served to third-party endpoints shall include direct source attribution links. Legacy Database Abstraction: The architecture shall decouple public data representations from underlying legacy hosting platforms using an abstraction layer.Automated Metadata Lineage: The system shall generate and maintain verifiably linked lineage metadata for all published statistical figures.Data Reconciliation Engine: The system shall automatically reconcile data variances between core operational and reporting databases (e.g., SIGMA and ODS).Machine Crawling Support: The data layer shall provide crawlable data endpoints optimized for automated AI agent indexing. Metadata Indexing: The system shall maintain real-time semantic indexes of statistical classifications and code lists.Automated Citation Generation: System responses shall auto-generate standardized persistent identifiers (DOIs) and citation strings. Non-Functional RequirementsDeterministic Data Grounding: Data outputs shall be strictly grounded in authoritative statistical sources to guarantee 100% deterministic outputs. Zero-Hallucination Assurance: The architecture shall enforce structural guardrails ensuring zero hallucination or statistical alteration in data served. Data Integrity: The system shall employ cryptographic hashing to verify data integrity during transit across dissemination channels.Data Synchronization Latency: Updates to core statistical repositories shall reflect in the machine-readable semantic layer within less than 1 second.Data Residency Compliance: All primary and backup data stores shall reside strictly within designated sovereign geographic boundaries. Scalable Data Ingestion: The data layer shall scale horizontally to handle high-throughput machine-to-machine querying without performance degradation. Metadata Completeness: 100% of published statistical tables shall be accompanied by standardized machine-readable metadata.3. Governance DomainFunctional RequirementsAlgorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA) Integration: The platform shall integrate the TBS Algorithmic Impact Assessment tool workflows into all system deployments. Unified Vision Policy Enforcement: The architecture shall enforce unified governance policies across conversational systems (e.g., CANChat and SSCAssistant).Interactive Taxonomy Mapping: The system shall map architectural components against an ArchiMate-based AI taxonomy metamodel.Centralized Requirements Traceability: The governance framework shall maintain end-to-end traceability across all 115 architectural requirements. Policy Engine Compliance Verification: The platform shall continuously scan configurations against the Policy on Service and Digital. Joint Declaration Audit Logs: System operations shall retain immutable logs validating compliance with organizational joint governance declarations. UNECE Framework Alignment: The architecture shall output governance metrics aligned with UNECE AI-readiness criteria.Service Authorization Tracking: System lifecycle dashboards shall track and document progress toward formal service authorizations. Vendor Claim Evaluation: The system shall evaluate third-party tools against standardized benchmark criteria to verify vendor claims. Non-Functional RequirementsFederal Directives Compliance: The system shall comply with all TBS directives, including Digital Sovereignty and Data Governance policies. Auditability: All administrative actions and systemic decisions shall be fully auditable by external oversight bodies. Multi-Layered Governance Alignment: Governance structures shall maintain strict vertical alignment across TOGAF layers (Motivation, Business, Application, Technology). International Code of Practice Standards: Dissemination controls shall align with the European Statistics Code of Practice and National Quality Assurance Frameworks. Sovereignty Governance: Governance protocols shall enforce strict rules governing hyper-scaler dependency and foreign jurisdiction risks. Policy Validation Latency: Automated compliance scans against infrastructure changes shall execute in under 5 minutes.Transparency: System governance frameworks shall publicly expose methodology documentation for automated processing workflows.4. Interoperability DomainFunctional RequirementsSemantic Layer Abstraction: The platform shall insert an explicit Semantic Layer within the TOGAF III-RM to bridge legacy content with modern AI tools. Universal Dissemination Protocol (USDP): The system shall implement USDP to support automated, machine-mediated statistical queries. OCI Standard Infrastructure Portability: Containerized workloads shall adhere to Open Container Initiative (OCI) specifications. CNCF Interface Support: Hosting environments shall utilize Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) container runtime interfaces. Legacy API Wrappers: The platform shall wrap legacy web publishing formats (HTML web tables) in machine-readable API protocols.Multi-Environment Orchestration: The architecture shall interoperate across on-premises, private cloud, and public hyper-scaler infrastructure. Cross-System Data Mapping: Semantic interfaces shall translate internal database formats directly into open standards like JSON-LD. Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Automation: Deployment definitions shall use IaC scripts (YAML, Python, Terraform) for interoperable environment creation. Automated Schema Translation: The system shall automatically transform SDMX metadata schemas into MCP-compliant formats. Vendor-Agnostic Container Runtime: The platform shall run containerized services without requiring proprietary cloud-provider extensions. Non-Functional RequirementsProtocol Backward Compatibility: The USDP layer shall support backward compatibility with legacy REST and SOAP endpoints.Interoperability Latency: Schema translation within the semantic layer shall add no more than 20 milliseconds to total request latency.Portability: Containerized applications shall be migratable across public and private cloud providers without code alteration. Open Standards Adherence: 100% of external integration interfaces shall rely on non-proprietary open standards. System Decoupling: Data, business, application, and technology layers shall remain loosely coupled to allow independent upgrades. Cross-Platform Consistency: Machine-readable metadata outputs shall remain identical regardless of underlying cloud deployment environments. Schema Validation Rate: The semantic layer shall achieve a 100% successful validation rate for outgoing JSON-LD and MCP payloads.5. Security DomainFunctional RequirementsITSG-33 Security Controls: The system shall implement security controls aligned with ITSG-33 guidelines. Static Code & Linting Analysis: Deployment pipelines shall run automated troubleshooting and security analysis using tools like Kube-linter and Terraform scanners. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): The platform shall enforce fine-grained RBAC across container namespaces and self-service portals. Sovereignty Isolation Guardrails: The architecture shall isolate public-facing AI interaction channels from core protected databases. Automated Vulnerability Scanning: Container images stored in cloud registries shall undergo continuous, automated vulnerability scanning. Immutable Audit Trails: System loggers shall record all machine-to-machine transactions in write-once-read-many (WORM) storage.Secret and Key Management: Automated IaC scripts shall retrieve sensitive credentials dynamically from secure vault stores. Traffic Sanitization: The protocol layer shall inspect and sanitize incoming machine traffic to prevent prompt injection or payload tampering.Isolated Testing Sandboxes: The architecture shall provide isolated proof-of-concept (PoC) sandboxes for testing delivery code.Non-Functional RequirementsSovereign Data Residency Protection: The security architecture shall prevent unauthorized data egress to foreign-controlled hyper-scalers. Zero-Trust Access Model: All inter-service communications across hybrid environments shall operate under a Zero-Trust network architecture.Resilience to Traffic Spikes: Machine dissemination channels shall withstand sudden automated machine crawling surges without collapsing underlying networks. Encryption at Rest and in Transit: 100% of data at rest and data in transit across all TOGAF layers shall be encrypted using AES-256 and TLS 1.3.Threat Mitigation Speed: Automated security pipelines shall block and isolate compromised container instances within less than 60 seconds.Identity Federation: The system shall federate access controls seamlessly with Government of Canada enterprise identity providers.Public Trust Safeguards: The system shall maintain cryptographic integrity to prevent unauthorized alteration of public statistics. 6. AI-Readiness DomainFunctional RequirementsMachine-Readable Metadata Exposure: The platform shall expose structural statistical metadata directly to third-party AI agents. Context-Aware Query Endpoints: The semantic layer shall accept dynamic context-aware queries from Large Language Models (LLMs).LLM Citation Generation: System interfaces shall provide LLMs with explicit citation templates to ensure outputs reference official agency sources. Machine-to-Machine Crawling Protocols: Dissemination channels shall provide optimized endpoints for automated machine crawling. Probabilistic Output Filtering: The semantic interface shall inspect probabilistic AI requests and return deterministic data payloads. AI Taxonomy Metamodel Tagging: The platform shall tag all hosted dataset services using standardized AI taxonomy metadata.Generative AI Guardrails: Architectural interfaces shall constrain generative AI agents from parsing uncontextualized HTML text.Automated Schema Mapping for LLMs: The system shall dynamically translate complex statistical multidimensional tables into flat MCP contexts. AI Model Context Binding: Statistical dissemination protocols shall bind published values to their associated quality notes and methodologies. Non-Functional RequirementsProbabilistic Risk Mitigation: The architecture shall eliminate the risk of variable or hallucinated responses when querying statistical figures. AI Discovery Speed: External AI agents shall discover, parse, and validate dataset availability via MCP/JSON-LD within less than 200 milliseconds. Machine Traffic Load Management: The USDP channels shall manage high-frequency AI agent requests without degrading performance for human users. Semantic Fidelity: Machine-readable translations (e.g., SDMX to JSON-LD) shall preserve 100% of original statistical semantics and relationships. Vendor-Agnostic LLM Support: The semantic layer shall operate uniformly regardless of whether queries originate from Commercial LLMs or internal assistants (e.g., CanChat, SSCAssistant). Model Interoperability Benchmark: 100% of public datasets shall satisfy target-state benchmarks for machine accessibility and semantic interoperability. Real-time Context Provisioning: The platform shall supply updated statistical contextual metadata to querying LLMs in real time.AI System Quality Grounding: System outputs shall be explicitly grounded in authoritative data sources to preserve institutional authority and public trust. Adaptability to Evolving AI Ecosystems: The architecture shall support new AI protocols and model interface standards via modular updates to the semantic layer.
